# How a ₹10 Cr+ home is bought in India, and what the site must do about it

Luxarea's website does not sell anything. It earns one action, a contact request, and it earns it by lowering perceived risk. Every section below exists because of one of these facts.

## 1. The person on the site is usually not the buyer

| Who | Role in the decision | What they need from the site | What makes them leave |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The principal**: promoter, CXO, doctor, second-generation business owner, NRI | Signs the cheque. Final yes or no. Rarely browses. | One line that says "these people are senior, discreet and know Mumbai". A fast way to reach a person. | Hype, stock photos, a form that feels like a call centre. |
| **The researcher**: spouse, son or daughter (25–35), executive assistant, family office | Shortlists, compares, books the visit. Most site traffic. | Something they can forward: a shareable floor link, clear specs, honest areas (carpet vs built-up), what happens next. | Dead ends ("Coming soon"), no prices at all, no way to share. |
| **The elders and advisers**: parents, CA, lawyer | Can veto. Ask about title, vastu, society, taxes, community. | Proof of due diligence, RERA numbers, a named office and registration. | Anything that looks unverified or fly-by-night. |
| **The NRI or Dubai buyer** | Buys remotely or across borders. | Video walkthroughs, help with POA and FEMA, for Dubai: LRS limits, Golden Visa, DLD fees. | Time-zone friction, phone-only contact. |

**Design consequences**
- Speak to the principal in the hero (discretion, seniority), and serve the researcher everywhere else (specs, sharing, process).
- The form asks **"Who is this for?"** (me / my family / someone I represent). Researchers are honest about it, and sales can send a forwardable shortlist instead of chasing the wrong person.
- Every residence floor has a **copyable link**, because the shortlist travels on WhatsApp to the person who decides.

## 2. The fear is not price. It's being burned.
At this price, buyers worry about title disputes, delayed possession, inflated carpet areas, society NOC problems, brokers leaking their interest to developers, and on land: CRZ, DP reservations and non-agricultural status. Trust is built by **showing the checklist**, not by claiming to be trustworthy.

- A "What we verify" section with real Indian terms (title chain, OC/CC, 7/12 extract, DP remarks, CRZ, DLD title deed). Using the correct vocabulary is itself a credibility signal.
- RERA agent registration in the footer and on every listing (and it's a legal requirement for agents in Maharashtra).
- A named BKC office address. A Bandra Kurla Complex address carries weight with this audience.
- A discretion pledge: number never shared with developers, off-market homes shown by invitation, NDA on request.

## 3. The decision is slow, social and offline
3–12 months, several site visits, family consensus. The site's job is to start that relationship, not to finish it.

- The primary CTA is **"Speak to an advisor"**, never "Buy" or "Book now".
- The process is spelled out with time frames, so the first call feels low-commitment: "20-minute call → shortlist in 48 hours → private viewings → due diligence → negotiation and registration".
- WhatsApp is the default channel in India for this segment. The form ends in WhatsApp (or a callback) instead of disappearing into an inbox.

## 4. Where people drop off, and the fixes
| Drop-off point | Fix on the site |
|---|---|
| Hero doesn't say who this is for within 5 seconds | One headline, one line of proof (25 years, BKC, Mumbai · Alibaug · Dubai), two buttons |
| Reaching contact takes more than 2 clicks | "Speak to an advisor" in the header on every page, a sticky WhatsApp and callback bar on mobile |
| Long forms | Two short steps: what you're looking for (taps, no typing), then name and WhatsApp. Four fields to type, at most |
| "What happens after I submit?" | Shown right under the button: who replies, on which channel, by when |
| Listings with no prices | Ranges ("from ₹16.8 Cr") or an explicit "Price shared on request" for off-market homes, never silence |
| Researchers can't share | Copy-link on every floor; deep links open straight to that floor |

## 5. What the two products are for
- **Home page**: builds trust, which produces contact requests. Measured by form submissions and WhatsApp starts.
- **Residence Finder** (the MapZ-style map and tower): lets people find what they're looking for in Luxarea's register in two or three clicks, then talk to a person. Every residence ends in "Request a private viewing" and "Copy link". Measured by residences opened and viewing requests.

## 6. Things Luxarea must supply before launch
Real listings and photos, founder names and photos, MahaRERA agent registration number, client stories (with permission), the reply-time promise they can actually keep, and their fee wording. Placeholders for all of these are marked in the build.
