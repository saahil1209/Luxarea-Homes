Due diligence

What we verify before you commit

At this price the risk is rarely the price. It is title, approvals and paperwork. Here is what we check, in plain terms.

Homes in Mumbai

Title chain
Ownership traced back through every sale, with your lawyer's title report.
MahaRERA registration
Project number, approved plans and declared possession date checked on MahaRERA.
OC and CC
Occupation and commencement certificates in place for the building and the floor.
Society NOC and dues
Society consent for transfer, and no maintenance or sinking-fund arrears.
Encumbrance
No mortgage, lien or pending litigation on the flat.
Measured carpet area
The RERA carpet area compared with what is actually built.

Land in Mumbai and Alibaug

7/12 extract
The land record showing owner, area and land use, matched to the seller.
Title search
A 30-year search for prior sales, mortgages and claims.
NA order
Non-agricultural permission in place, since agricultural land is generally limited to agriculturists.
DP remarks
The Development Plan zoning, reservations and proposed roads affecting the plot.
CRZ
Whether coastal regulation limits what you can build, which matters in Alibaug.
FSI and TDR
How much you can build today, and what more is possible.

Property in Dubai

DLD title deed
Ownership confirmed with the Dubai Land Department, or the Oqood registration for off-plan.
Escrow account
Off-plan payments go into the project's registered escrow account, not to the developer directly.
Developer track record
Past projects, delivery delays and handover quality.
Golden Visa eligibility
Whether the purchase qualifies under the rules at the time you apply.
LRS limits
Remittance planned within each family member's USD 250,000 annual limit, with TCS accounted for.
Service charges
Annual community charges and likely rental yield, so the numbers are real.

Questions

What buyers ask us first

Can NRIs and OCI card holders buy property in India?

Yes. NRIs and OCI card holders can buy residential and commercial property in India without RBI approval. They cannot buy agricultural land, plantation property or farmhouses. Payment must come from abroad through normal banking channels or from an NRE, NRO or FCNR account.

How much can a resident Indian send abroad to buy property in Dubai?

Under the RBI's Liberalised Remittance Scheme, each resident individual can remit up to USD 250,000 per financial year, so family members can pool their limits. Tax collected at source (TCS) applies to remittances above ₹10 lakh in a year and can be claimed against your income tax.

Does buying property in Dubai qualify for the UAE Golden Visa?

Property worth AED 2 million or more can qualify an investor for the 10-year UAE Golden Visa. The rules that apply are those in force when you apply, so we check eligibility for your specific purchase before you commit.

Can I buy agricultural land near Mumbai or in Alibaug?

In Maharashtra, agricultural land can generally be bought only by agriculturists, so most buyers purchase non-agricultural (NA) plots. Before any purchase we verify the NA order, the 7/12 extract, the title chain and whether the plot falls under coastal regulation (CRZ).

How does Luxarea keep my search private?

Your name and number are never shared with developers or other brokers. Off-market homes are shown by invitation, under NDA if you prefer, and one senior advisor handles your search from start to finish.

Do you help with home loans?

Yes. We connect buyers with reputable banks and financial institutions, and coordinate the loan with the purchase timeline.

Can you help me sell or lease my property?

Yes. Share the location, size and key features, and we will value it, advise on pricing, and present it discreetly to qualified buyers or tenants. Most residential leases in Mumbai run for 11 months, renewable by agreement.

Our fee is explained in writing at the first meeting, before you see a single home.
