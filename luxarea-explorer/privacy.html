Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This policy explains how Luxarea Homes Private Limited ("Luxarea", "we", "us"), Parinee Crescenzo, 1B, 1st Floor, G Block, Bandra Kurla Complex, Bandra East, Mumbai 400051, handles personal data you share through this website. We act as the data fiduciary for that data.

What we collect

  • What you tell us in an enquiry: your name, WhatsApp or mobile number, how you prefer to be contacted, what you are looking for, your budget range, who the search is for, and any note you add.
  • Context about the enquiry: the page you sent it from, the website that referred you, and campaign tags in the link (for example utm_source), so we know which pages help people reach us.
  • Messages and calls you choose to have with us on WhatsApp, by phone or by email.

We do not ask for payment details, identity documents or passwords on this website.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and advise you on the homes, land or property you asked about.
  • To arrange viewings, share shortlists and keep a record of your search.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations, including those under RERA and tax law.

We use your data on the basis of the consent you give when you tick the box on our form, and you can withdraw it at any time (see "Your rights" below).

Who we share it with

We do not share your name or number with developers, other brokers or marketing lists. We share data only:

  • with the service providers we use to communicate and keep records, such as WhatsApp and our email provider, under their own privacy terms;
  • with a seller, developer, lawyer, bank or other party in a transaction, only when you ask us to and only what is needed;
  • when the law requires it, for example in response to a valid request from a court or government authority.

Cookies and third-party services

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It remembers an off-market invitation code for the length of your visit in your browser's session storage. To show maps and fonts, your browser loads files from OpenFreeMap and Google Fonts, which receive your IP address as part of that request. If you send an enquiry by WhatsApp, WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to that message.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry data for as long as we are advising you and for up to three years after our last contact, unless the law requires us to keep it longer (for example transaction records), or you ask us to delete it sooner.

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal data we hold about you, correct or update it, delete it, or stop contacting you. You can also withdraw your consent at any time; this does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it. Write to us at %BRAND.email% and we will respond within 30 days.

Grievances

If you have a concern about how we handle your data, email %BRAND.email% with the subject "Privacy grievance", or write to us at the office address above. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data, and limit access to the people at Luxarea who are advising you. No method of sending or storing data over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

This website is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
